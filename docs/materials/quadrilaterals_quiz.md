# Quadrilaterals and Their Properties Quiz

## Multiple-Choice Questions

1. **[Easy]** What is the sum of the interior angles of any quadrilateral?
   A) $180^\circ$
   B) $270^\circ$
   C) $360^\circ$
   D) $540^\circ$

2. **[Easy]** Which of the following statements is ALWAYS true about a rectangle?
   A) All four sides are of equal length.
   B) The diagonals are perpendicular to each other.
   C) The diagonals are equal in length and bisect each other.
   D) The diagonals divide the angles into $45^\circ$ halves.

3. **[Easy]** What is the defining property of a trapezium?
   A) Opposite sides are equal in length.
   B) It has at least one pair of parallel opposite sides.
   C) Diagonals bisect each other at $90^\circ$.
   D) Opposite angles are always supplementary.

4. **[Easy]** In an isosceles trapezium, which of the following is true?
   A) All four sides are of equal length.
   B) The non-parallel sides have equal lengths.
   C) Opposite angles are always equal.
   D) The diagonals do not intersect.

5. **[Medium]** In parallelogram $ABCD$, if the angle $\angle A$ is $40^\circ$, what is the measure of the adjacent angle $\angle B$?
   A) $40^\circ$
   B) $50^\circ$
   C) $140^\circ$
   D) $150^\circ$

6. **[Medium]** If the diagonals of a quadrilateral are equal in length and bisect each other, what is the most general name for this quadrilateral?
   A) Square
   B) Rhombus
   C) Rectangle
   D) Parallelogram

7. **[Medium]** Which quadrilateral is defined as having two non-overlapping adjacent pairs of sides of equal length?
   A) Parallelogram
   B) Rhombus
   C) Kite
   D) Trapezium

8. **[Medium]** In a rhombus $GAME$, if the diagonals intersect at point $O$, what is the measure of the angle $\angle GOE$?
   A) $45^\circ$
   B) $60^\circ$
   C) $90^\circ$
   D) $120^\circ$

9. **[Hard]** Let $CASE$ be a square. If the midpoints of its sides are joined in order to form a new quadrilateral $UVWX$, what type of quadrilateral is $UVWX$?
   A) Rectangle but not a square
   B) Rhombus but not a square
   C) Square
   D) Parallelogram but not a rectangle

10. **[Hard]** Which of the following statements is FALSE?
    A) Every square is a rhombus.
    B) Every rhombus is a kite.
    C) Every rectangle is a parallelogram.
    D) Every isosceles trapezium is a parallelogram.

## Answer Key

1. **C** — The sum of all angles in any quadrilateral is $360^\circ$.
2. **C** — By definition, a rectangle's diagonals are equal and bisect each other.
3. **B** — A trapezium is a quadrilateral with at least one pair of parallel opposite sides.
4. **B** — When the non-parallel sides of a trapezium have the same lengths, it is called an isosceles trapezium.
5. **C** — In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are supplementary (add up to $180^\circ$). So, $\angle B = 180^\circ - 40^\circ = 140^\circ$.
6. **C** — If diagonals are equal and bisect each other, the shape must be a rectangle. If they also intersected at $90^\circ$, it would be a square.
7. **C** — A kite is a quadrilateral where adjacent sides have the same length.
8. **C** — The diagonals of a rhombus always bisect each other at right angles ($90^\circ$).
9. **C** — Joining the midpoints of a square's sides forms another square. All sides are equal to $x\sqrt{2}$ and all angles are $90^\circ$.
10. **D** — An isosceles trapezium has only one pair of parallel opposite sides, whereas a parallelogram must have two pairs of parallel opposite sides. Therefore, an isosceles trapezium is not a parallelogram.
