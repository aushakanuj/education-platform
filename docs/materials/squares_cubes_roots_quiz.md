# Squares, Cubes, and Their Roots Quiz

## Multiple-Choice Questions

1. **[Easy]** Which of the following numbers can be immediately identified as NOT a perfect square by looking only at its units digit?
   A) 1089
   B) 2048
   C) 2025
   D) 1296

2. **[Easy]** What is the value of the cube of $-5$ (i.e., $(-5)^3$)?
   A) $-15$
   B) $-25$
   C) $-125$
   D) 125

3. **[Easy]** What is the positive square root of 441?
   A) 11
   B) 21
   C) 31
   D) 41

4. **[Easy]** How many zeros are at the end of the square of a number that ends with 3000 (three zeros)?
   A) Three zeros
   B) Five zeros
   C) Six zeros
   D) Nine zeros

5. **[Medium]** Find the value of $\sqrt{324}$ using the prime factorization method.
   A) 12
   B) 16
   C) 18
   D) 24

6. **[Medium]** If the square of a number is 1225 (which is the sum of the first 35 consecutive odd numbers starting from 1), what is the value of $36^2$?
   A) 1296
   B) 1261
   C) 1350
   D) 1440

7. **[Medium]** Which of the following is the Hardy-Ramanujan number, known as the smallest number that can be expressed as the sum of two cubes in two different ways?
   A) 1331
   B) 1729
   C) 4104
   D) 13832

8. **[Medium]** What is the cube root of 10648?
   A) 18
   B) 22
   C) 28
   D) 32

9. **[Hard]** What is the smallest square number that is divisible by each of the numbers 4, 9, and 10?
   A) 180
   B) 360
   C) 900
   D) 3600

10. **[Hard]** Find the smallest number by which 1323 must be multiplied so that the product becomes a perfect cube.
    A) 3
    B) 7
    C) 9
    D) 21

## Answer Key

1. **B** — Perfect squares cannot end with 2, 3, 7, or 8. Since 2048 ends in 8, it cannot be a perfect square.
2. **C** — $(-5)^3 = (-5) \times (-5) \times (-5) = 25 \times (-5) = -125$.
3. **B** — Since $21 \times 21 = 441$, the square root of 441 is 21.
4. **C** — The number of zeros at the end of a square is double the number of zeros at the end of the original number. Three zeros doubled is six zeros.
5. **C** — $324 = (2 \times 2) \times (3 \times 3) \times (3 \times 3) = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 3^2$. Taking one factor from each pair gives $2 \times 3 \times 3 = 18$.
6. **A** — To find $36^2$, we add the 36th odd number ($2n - 1 = 2(36) - 1 = 71$) to $35^2$ (1225). Thus, $1225 + 71 = 1296$.
7. **B** — 1729 is the smallest taxicab number, written as $1^3 + 12^3$ and $9^3 + 10^3$.
8. **B** — By prime factorization or estimation, $22^3 = 10648$. Thus, the cube root of 10648 is 22.
9. **C** — The LCM of 4, 9, and 10 is 180. The prime factorization of $180 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5$. To make it a perfect square, we must multiply by 5 to pair the factor 5. $180 \times 5 = 900$, which is $30^2$.
10. **B** — $1323 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7 \times 7$. To form triplets, we have three 3s but only two 7s. We need one more 7 to complete the triplet of 7s.
