# Square Numbers and Odd Number Sums Quiz

## Multiple-Choice Questions

1. **[Easy]** Which of the following numbers can be immediately identified as NOT a perfect square by looking only at its units digit?
   A) 1296
   B) 1027
   C) 2025
   D) 1089

2. **[Easy]** What is the value of the square of a number that ends with 400 (two zeros)?
   A) 1600
   B) 16000
   C) 160000
   D) 1600000

3. **[Easy]** Which of the following represents the sum of the first 5 consecutive odd numbers starting from 1?
   A) 10
   B) 15
   C) 25
   D) 35

4. **[Easy]** If the area of a square field is $441\text{ m}^2$, what is the length of its side?
   A) $11\text{ m}$
   B) $21\text{ m}$
   C) $31\text{ m}$
   D) $41\text{ m}$

5. **[Medium]** Given that $35^2 = 1225$, what is the value of $36^2$ using the consecutive odd number pattern?
   A) 1261
   B) 1296
   C) 1325
   D) 1350

6. **[Medium]** If we add the two consecutive triangular numbers 10 and 15, what perfect square do we obtain?
   A) 9
   B) 16
   C) 25
   D) 36

7. **[Medium]** Using successive subtraction of consecutive odd numbers starting from 1, how many steps does it take to find the square root of 81?
   A) 7
   B) 8
   C) 9
   D) 10

8. **[Medium]** Find the square root of 324 using the prime factorization method?
   A) 12
   B) 16
   C) 18
   D) 22

9. **[Hard]** What is the smallest perfect square number that is divisible by each of the numbers 4, 9, and 10?
   A) 180
   B) 360
   C) 900
   D) 3600

10. **[Hard]** Estimate the positive square root of 1936. If $40^2 = 1600$, $50^2 = 2500$, and $45^2 = 2025$, what is the exact square root?
    A) 42
    B) 44
    C) 46
    D) 48

## Answer Key

1. **B** — A perfect square can never end in 2, 3, 7, or 8. Since 1027 ends in 7, it is definitely not a perfect square.
2. **C** — Since the number of ending zeros doubles when we square a number, a number with 2 zeros will have 4 zeros in its square. $400 \times 400 = 160000$.
3. **C** — The sum of the first $n$ consecutive odd numbers starting from 1 is $n^2$. For $n = 5$, the sum is $5^2 = 25$.
4. **B** — The side of the square is the square root of its area. Since $21 \times 21 = 441$, the side length is $21\text{ m}$.
5. **B** — To find $36^2$ from $35^2 = 1225$, we add the 36th odd number, which is $2n - 1 = 2(36) - 1 = 71$. Thus, $1225 + 71 = 1296$.
6. **C** — Adding consecutive triangular numbers 10 and 15 gives $10 + 15 = 25$, which is $5^2$.
7. **C** — Successively subtracting $1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17$ from 81 takes exactly 9 steps to reach 0, showing that $\sqrt{81} = 9$.
8. **C** — The prime factorization of $324 = (2 \times 2) \times (3 \times 3) \times (3 \times 3) = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 3^2$. Taking one factor from each pair gives $2 \times 3 \times 3 = 18$.
9. **C** — The Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 4, 9, and 10 is 180. The prime factorization of $180 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5$. To make it a perfect square, we multiply by 5 to pair the factor 5, which gives $180 \times 5 = 900$.
10. **B** — Since 1936 is between 1600 ($40^2$) and 2025 ($45^2$), its square root is between 40 and 45. Since 1936 ends in 6, the units digit must be 4, which leaves 44 as the only candidate. $44 \times 44 = 1936$.
